# Surface brief: home page (getgymledger.com)

Scope: the landing page (app/page.tsx) and the site chrome it sets (header, footer, buttons, tokens) that the blog, support and legal pages inherit. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: lifters choosing an iPhone workout tracker, mostly arriving on a phone. Action: tap through to the App Store. Proof on hand: six real GymLedger 2.0 screenshots, the App Store description, US prices. No ratings, reviews or user counts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the gym whiteboard you read before you lift: today's workout written in marker, the real app held up by magnets, and an eraser that wipes one day's board into the next. It refuses the category default of a dark page, centred phone mockup and icon-card feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool glossy board white with a faint printed grid; black, red (brand), blue and green dry-erase marker; ghosted residue of erased words; brushed-aluminium frame and tray; round magnets; printed sheets held up by magnets for the blog. Marker face for single words, numbers and annotations; a plate-lettering grotesk (Barlow, Barlow Condensed for printed labels and numbers) for sentences. No cards, no gradients, no glass.

STORY: Visitors see today's workout already written up (the app picks the next session in your rotation), wipe the board to see Push, Pull and Legs become logging, picking and building, then read the board's other sections: recovery as a dated countdown, protein as a tally, PRs on a PR board, the rest timer on the Lock Screen, the checklist of everything else, the price tags, the reading list. They leave believing one app covers training, recovery and protein, and tap Download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Aluminium-framed board. Header strip: app-icon magnet + GymLedger left; Blog, Support and a black Download button with the real Apple logo right. Left 7 of 12 columns: "Today:" in black marker, the day's word ("PULL") in red marker at 18vw (never shrinks below 120px), the H1 sentence in Barlow 800 beneath it, one supporting line, then the App Store button. Right 5 columns: the real iPhone screenshot for that day, tilted, held by two magnets, with blue-marker callouts and drawn arrows. Bottom: the aluminium tray holding the eraser (the control), a red marker and a "free trial" note.

FORM: The Gym Whiteboard, position 3 on my ordered list (anatomy wall chart, competition plates and attempt board, gym whiteboard, training ledger, Nutrition Facts panel, Munich '72 identity, rubber floor and chalk). Seed key d264e0b9. Raises: the day's word never shrinks; one eraser gesture rewrites the board; states are written as marker notes, never chrome badges; the faint grid governs alignment; recovery reads as a dated countdown.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: drag (or press) the eraser in the tray to wipe the board; the day's word and list erase left to right, leave a ghost, and the next day writes itself in while the phone swaps to that day's screen (Pull: dashboard, Legs: workout editor, Push: live session). Section words write themselves in once on first view. Reduced motion: instant swap, no write-on.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
